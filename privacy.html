eBay AutoResponder Privacy Policy

Updated November 6, 2026.

Purpose and information used

This private, self-hosted application helps its operator manage authorized eBay seller messaging. During the current setup phase, it reads seller identity, conversation metadata and a limited message sample to validate API access. Read-only history checks do not retain buyer message bodies. During a controlled notification test, a bounded number of signed payloads may be encrypted temporarily in the local database for signature, routing and replay validation. They are purged after the test, or within approximately ten minutes if the test is interrupted; expired captures are also purged when the service restarts. Metadata hashes and test outcomes may be retained. Production automated replies are not enabled.

Storage and access

Seller account identifiers and encrypted OAuth refresh tokens are stored in the operator's local database. Temporary OAuth security cookies expire after ten minutes. Access is limited to the homelab operator. Message content is not sent to hosted AI services, sold, or shared for advertising.

Service providers

eBay handles account sign-in and authorization. Cloudflare carries encrypted web traffic to this self-hosted service and may process network request metadata. GitHub Pages hosts this public policy and may process visitor network metadata. This policy page does not set cookies or use analytics.

Revocation, retention and deletion

The seller can revoke authorization in eBay's Third-party app access settings. Account identifiers and credentials are retained while the connection is needed. The controlled seller can contact their homelab operator through their existing contact channel to request local account and credential deletion. Revocation at eBay and deletion of local records are separate actions.

This policy will be updated before additional data processing is enabled.
